# RECIPES.md — the curated recipe bank

Dishes the user saved (from Instagram reels) that the weekly generator MUST draw
from before inventing anything. Each entry lists the source, the core recipe as
posted, and the ADAPTATIONS needed to fit MEAL_PLAN.md (uric-acid safety, low
sodium, no oven/grill/air-fryer, no sugar/maida, S/V portions). A dish marked
"needs adaptation" must only appear in week.json in its adapted form.

Counts still apply: chicken max 3 meals/week, fish max 2 (same fish), no protein
twice in a day. Use a bank recipe at most once per week; rotate across weeks.

## Soya & plant protein

### 1. Soya Chukauni (curd-tadka soya chunks)
- Source: instagram.com/reel/DdtdgZHo2S4 (Dr Pal / @myflavourfuljourney), also instagram.com/reel/Ddy2Czhi15t (@simarcookss)
- As posted: boil soya chunks, sauté with turmeric/chilli/coriander/ginger-garlic + onion; mix Greek yogurt with salt + raw garlic; tadka of mustard oil, turmeric, chilli, kasuri methi poured into the yogurt; combine off the flame. Serve with rice.
- Adaptations: tadka oil 3 tbsp → 1 tbsp; salt light; pair with ⅓ bowl matta/brown rice (never white). Off-flame yogurt mixing keeps it creamy.
- Slot: lunch or dinner. Protein tag: soya (+ curd base).

- Source: instagram.com/reel/DbF_BdnAIh3 (@prasannaspalate_)
